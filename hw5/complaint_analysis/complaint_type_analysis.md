# Task 3

To find which complaint type to plot, I ran `complaint_borough.py` on January 1 to February 29, 2024 and added up the counts across boroughs. HEAT/HOT WATER came out on top, so that's the one I used.

The bar chart in `complaint_analysis.ipynb` shows the heat/hot water counts per borough for January-February next to June-July. In every borough there are far more complaints in January-February than in June-July.

This makes sense. January and February are the coldest months of the year, so that's when people actually need their heating and notice right away when it breaks. In June and July nobody is running the heat, so the few complaints that are left are probably mostly about hot water.
